Requirements (Instruments, Chemicals & Other)<br>
Butter sample<br>
Neutralized ethanol<br>
Sodium hydroxide solution (0.1 N)<br>
Phenolphthalein indicator<br>
Burette with stand<br>
Conical flask (250 ml)<br>
Weighing balance<br>
Hot plate<br>

Procedure  <br>
1.	Weigh accurately 5 g of butter sample into a 250 ml conical flask.<br>
2.	Add 50 ml of neutralized ethanol into the flask.<br>
3.	Heat the flask gently at about 50°C to dissolve the butter completely.<br>
4.	Add 2 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the mixture.<br>
5.	Titrate the hot solution with 0.1 N NaOH solution until a light pink color persists.<br>
6.	Note the burette reading and calculate the FFA content<br>

Calculation<br>
FFA content (% Oleic acid) = (V × N × 0.282) / W × 100<br>

Where,<br>
V = Volume of NaOH used (ml)<br>
N = Normality of NaOH solution (0.1 N)<br>
W = Weight of butter sample (g)<br>
0.282 = Milliequivalent weight of oleic acid.<br>
